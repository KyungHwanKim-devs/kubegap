# Shape of summary.json (kubegap reference)

The key shape of the summary file, moved here from SKILL.md section 5. One section per heading. `{}` is a dict, `[]` a list, `(…)` an explanation. The `each` paths of checks (`collectors/*/checks.yaml`) point at these keys.

## inventory

`inventory` (the essentials of inventory.json)

## events

`events{count, by_type, reasons[{reason, type, events, occurrences, first, last, objects_top[]}], top_objects[], warning_samples{reason: [...]}}`

## metrics

`metrics{<query-id>: {source, why, ok, series, stats{min,max,mean,n}, top[{labels, value}], truncated}}`(`top-nodes` and `top-pods` use `head[]`)

## objects

`objects{kinds{}, namespaces{}, pods{by_phase, restarts_top[], pending[], waiting[], failed[], qos}, deployments{unavailable[], replicas_1, replicas_0}, services{by_type, no_ready_endpoints[]}, nodes{not_ready[], pressure[], unschedulable[]}}`

## recent_changes

`recent_changes{hours, workloads[], namespaces[], event_targets[]}`, `events.history{tracked_hits[{reason, occurrences, per_snapshot[], trend, objects_top[]}], tracked_silent[]}`(accumulated across snapshots)

## conditions

`conditions{unhealthy[{kind, namespace, name, condition, status, reason, message, age_hours}]}`

## webhooks

`webhooks{webhooks[], no_endpoints[], self_lock_risk[]}`

## observability

`observability{rules{alerting, by_class, symptom_rules[], app_rules[], page_severity_cause[]}, alerts{never_fired_15d[], always_firing_7d[], firing_now[], top_firing_7d[]}, failure_modes{matrix[], uncovered[]}, slo{...}, alertmanager{default_receiver, receivers, routes[], silences_active[], alerts_by_state, notifications_failed_1d[], config_parse_error(only when the Alertmanager config failed to parse)}, golden_signals{rows[], missing_lte[], alb_error(only when aws/alb-metrics.json could not be read)}, targets{down[], dropped}, grafana{version, database, datasources, dashboards, dashboards_json{available, read, rows[{uid, title, panels_with_expr, metrics, folder}], panel_metrics, alert_metrics, alert_metrics_without_panel[], alert_metrics_without_panel_count, empty_dashboards[]}}}`(`dashboards_json` is `available: true` only when a `grafana_api_token` was configured and `grafana/dashboards/<uid>.json` was fetched)
