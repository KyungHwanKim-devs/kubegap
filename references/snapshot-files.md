# Files in a snapshot folder (kubegap reference)

Moved here from SKILL.md section 5. One snapshot (`<snapshot root>/<context>/<YYYY-MM-DDTHH-MM-SS>/`) holds the files below. The key shape of `summary.json` is in [summary-shape.md](summary-shape.md).

- `inventory.json`: dict. `cluster{platform, platform_reasons, server_version, eks{version, control_plane_logging, control_plane_logging_off, endpoint_public, public_access_cidrs, secrets_encryption, support_type, addons{<name>: {version,status}}, nodegroups}}`, `profile{name, source, reasons}`, `node_access{method, ssm_registered[], ssm_unregistered[]}`, `nodes{count, ready, kubelet_versions[], os_images[], kernels[], runtimes[], architectures[], zones[], capacity_types[], nodes[{name, roles, instance_type, zone, capacity_type, nodepool, internal_ip, allocatable, capacity}]}`, `namespaces{count, names[], prod_like[], dev_like[]}`, `pods{count, by_phase}`, `storage_classes[]`, `crds{count, groups[]}`, `components{<name>: {present, family, evidence}}`, `components_present[]`, `families_present[]`, `missing_families[]`, `stores{prometheus|alertmanager|loki|grafana: {ok, version, retention, service}}`, `collectors_enabled[]`.
- `findings-draft.json`: dict. `findings` is a **list** (sorted by severity). Item: `id, check, name, area, type, status(fail|warn), severity_default, symptom_linked, target, evidence(string or list), instances, source, why, suggest, verify[], cases[], notes, thresholds`. `check_results` is also a **list** (not a dict): `id, status(done|disabled|skip|error|tool-missing), counts{fail,warn,pass,excluded}, reason, thresholds`. Also `checks{total, by_status, load_errors}`, `findings_count`, `suppressed[]`, `expired_suppressions[]`, `llm_checks[]`, `severity_rules{levels, rules[]}`, `rubric_version`, `profile`.
- `diff.json`: dict. `inventory{...changes}`, `objects{kinds_compared, added[], removed[], changed[{kind,name,generation,changed_fields}], count_only{}, summary}`, `findings{counts_before, counts_after, new[], resolved[], changed[], rename_suspects[], summary, persisted{runs_checked, max_runs, persisted_3plus, persisted_all, toil_candidates_by_check{<check-id>: n}, rows[{id, runs, check, status, severity_default}], note}}` (`persisted` = walk back through up to 10 earlier snapshots of the same context and count how many consecutive times this finding appeared — material for the Toil candidates in the report), `events{changed[], warning_reasons_new[]}`, `metrics{<query-id>: {series, appeared, disappeared, top_deltas[]}}`.
- `objects/<kind>.json`: kubectl's `{"items": [...]}` as-is. `events.json` is the same. `metrics/<id>.json`: `{id, query, why, ok, result[{metric{}, value[ts, "value"]}]}`.
- `tools/helm-values/<ns>__<release>.json`: `helm get values -o json` (user values; `null` if none). `grafana/dashboards/<uid>.json`: Grafana `/api/dashboards/uid/<uid>` (only with a token). `aws/acm-certificates.json`, `aws/service-quotas-<service>.json`, `aws/cw-quota-usage.json`, `aws/ec2-root-volumes.json`, `aws/cw-ebs-volumes.json`: results of the EKS platform collector's ACM, Service Quotas and generic CloudWatch step (`cw-metrics`, `lib/cwmetrics.py`). Summarized in `summary.eksplatform`.
