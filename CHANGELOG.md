# Changelog

All notable changes to kubegap are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- `resources.cpu-request-overprovisioned` and `resources.memory-request-overprovisioned` no longer fail a database, cache or message queue container; it stays at warn and the evidence says "database-like". A Neo4j StatefulSet at 1% of its 500m CPU request and 19% of its 4 GB memory request used to fail both, and the report advised cutting the requests to the 7-day p95, which a database cannot live with: backup, VACUUM, compaction and recovery take many times the quiet-hour CPU, and the memory is its cache. The container is judged by its name, image repository and workload name. The detection comes from `resilience.db-probe-timeout` and is now shared as `lib.resilience.is_db_like` and the `db_like(labels)` helper for metric checks, with two changes that also reach the probe rows: the exclusion for exporters, operators, UIs and the like now reads the image repository as well and knows a few more helper names, so the metrics exporter next to a Redis is no longer a database; and a container named for a database counts even under a StatefulSet called `kafka-controller`, as Bitnami names its KRaft brokers. `references/report-writing.md` gains the writing rule never to advise cutting such requests to the p95, and `analysis-procedure.md` step 9 points to it. On the 2026-10-27 EKS snapshot the two Neo4j findings go from fail to warn, 13 CPU and 5 memory findings carry the mark, the probe rows of `kafka-controller:kafka` and `redis-master:metrics` swap their `db_like` value with no probe flag changing, and no other finding changes. Rubric 0.1.63. ([#6](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/6))
- The severity rule for the four absences, no SLO, no symptom alerts, no backup tool and N+1 not met, no longer says "critical regardless of profile". Each of those checks lowers itself to warn when it finds a mitigating signal, such as a node autoscaler for N+1 or etcd snapshots and backup CronJobs for backup, and the old rule raised the item back to critical; three of the five criticals in the 2026-10-26 EKS full report came from it, and a k3s cluster doing exactly what the backup check suggests was still critical. The rule now reads: fail is critical, warn is high, raising a warn back needs a reason in `adjustments.md`, and the profile-context rule applies. `resilience.n-plus-one` moves from high to critical as its default so that its fail, no autoscaler and no room, carries the same severity as the other three. `rubric/severity.yaml` states at the top that checks and rules do not see each other and that a rule never undoes a check's warn. Rubric 0.1.62. ([#16](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/16))
- `traffic.ingress-p95-latency` and `traffic.ingress-5xx-ratio` no longer pass a host for being quiet. Both required 100 requests in the window before saying anything, so a host with a 34 s p95 on 77 requests, or one that answered 1 of its 4 requests with a 5xx, was a pass. The 100-request minimum now applies to fail only: latency warns from a 1 s p95 at any request count, and the 5xx ratio warns from 5% under 100 requests, for the ALB and the ingress-nginx source alike. The evidence says "under 100 requests: warn only" in that case. On the 2026-10-27 EKS snapshot this adds four warns, among them a host with a 22 s p95 and a 25% 5xx ratio on 4 requests. Rubric 0.1.61. ([#12](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/12))
- The daily report ranks its Top 3 in a new order: new or recurred items that still have impact now, then the persisting critical and high items with the largest impact, then events that have ended. Before, "new or recurred first" put an hour-long p95 spike that was already over above a spot-node concentration that had caused four outages that day. An ended event that still makes the list says so in its title or first sentence. To make "still going on" checkable from the summary, the traffic summary now carries the latest hourly ALB p95 as `p95_s_last_1h` on load balancers, target groups and Ingresses, and `traffic.ingress-p95-latency` prints it in its evidence as `latest 1h`; the 24-hour average that the check judges is an average of hourly p95 values, so one bad hour can carry it over the threshold while the service is fine now. Ingresses measured through ingress-nginx get the same key with their single 1-hour value. `references/summary-shape.md` lists the key. Rubric 0.1.60. ([#8](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/8))

### Fixed

- `lib/yamlite.py` raised "not a `key: value` entry" on a bare `-` at the key's indent on the line after `key:`, as in `a:\n-\n- y`, while `- x` in the same place loaded. The same-indent list branch now accepts the bare dash too, so a list that starts with an empty item loads as `{"a": [None, "y"]}`, as in PyYAML. Found while comparing [#69](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/pull/69) with PyYAML.
- `lib/yamlite.py` read a same-indent `- w` on the line after an empty sequence item `-` as a nested sequence, so `-\n- w` loaded as `[["w"]]` instead of `[None, "w"]`. The parser now knows whether it came from a mapping key or a sequence dash; only after `key:` does a same-indent `- ` start a list, and after `-` it is the next item, as in PyYAML. Fixed by [@ShahadatTest](https://github.com/ShahadatTest) in [#69](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/pull/69). ([#66](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/66))
- `lib/yamlite.py` says what it does not support and on which line. A plain string continued on a deeper line used to fail with "mapping indentation mismatch" or "list indentation mismatch"; the error now names the continuation line and says that multi-line plain scalars are not supported, also for a scalar that starts on the line after `key:`, which now loads when it stays on one line. An anchor or tag on a key, such as `&a key: v`, was read into the key name and now raises like one on a value. `CONTRIBUTING.md` links the supported subset. Fixed by [@RecordK](https://github.com/RecordK) in [#52](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/pull/52). ([#26](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/26))

## [0.1.2] - 2026-11-02

The repository hygiene milestone is complete: public incident cases are attached to the checks, and measurements are kept apart from them. Four checks came out of that case work. Contributors to this release: [@MinwooPark93](https://github.com/MinwooPark93) (#48) and [@hsgustn](https://github.com/hsgustn) (#49).

### Added

- `vpccni.snat-port-randomization-off`: warns when the node SNAT rule does not use iptables `--random-fully` for source ports, with external SNAT off: `AWS_VPC_K8S_CNI_RANDOMIZESNAT` set to `none` or `hashrandom`, a VPC CNI below 1.7.0 with the setting unset, or an image without a version. Pods leaving the VPC then race on conntrack inserts and connection setup shows exact 1 s and 3 s delays, the Prezi 2019 incident. The VPC CNI summary records the setting and the resulting mode. Rubric 0.1.59, 407 checks. ([#47](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/47))
- `traffic.ingress-nginx-vulnerable-version` and `traffic.ingress-nginx-retired`. The first fails when the ingress-nginx controller is below the IngressNightmare fix, 1.12.1 or 1.11.5, and the admission webhook is on, which lets any pod in the cluster run code in the controller and read every Secret; it warns when the webhook is off. The second warns whenever ingress-nginx is deployed: the project stopped in March 2026 with no further releases or security fixes, so the finding stays until the move to a Gateway API implementation is done. The traffic summary now records the controller's image version, replicas and admission webhook. Rubric 0.1.58, 406 checks. ([#46](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/46))
- `hygiene.configmap-changed-after-pod-start`: warns when a ConfigMap was changed after the pods that read it through env, envFrom or a subPath mount started, so the running pods still hold the old value and the change will apply at the next unrelated restart. Plain file mounts are refreshed by the kubelet and are listed as refresh-only. The change time comes from `managedFields`, so discover now fetches ConfigMaps with `--show-managed-fields`; snapshots without it skip the check as not collected. Secrets are not collected and not judged. Rubric 0.1.56, 404 checks. ([#45](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/45))
- `vpccni.external-snat-off-with-private-routes`: warns when the VPC CNI keeps its default of SNATing pod traffic that leaves the VPC while the cluster VPC has routes to a peering connection, a transit gateway or a VPN. On those paths the destination sees the node IP instead of the pod IP, which is what cost MindTickle a month of lost packets in 2020. The full run now collects the VPC route tables, `aws/describe-route-tables.json`, through the `ec2:Describe*` permission the IAM policy already grants. Rubric 0.1.54, 403 checks. ([#43](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/43))

### Changed

- Measurements taken on the clusters a check was written on now live in a new `measured` field, apart from `cases`. They had been written into `why` on 198 checks, and the report rule let a report take a case from `why`, so the Cases line of `resilience.prod-concentration`, `hygiene.mutable-image-tag` and `prometheus.storage-risk` printed the author's own cluster as if it were a public incident. A report now takes cases from `cases` only and may add a separate "Measured when this check was written" line. A test keeps measurements out of `why` and `notes`. Rubric 0.1.57. Reported and fixed by [@hsgustn](https://github.com/hsgustn) in [#49](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/pull/49). ([#17](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/17))
- 108 checks now carry `cases`, public incident write-ups whose root cause is the condition the check detects, 203 links in all; before, 2 checks did. The sources are the 59 stories on k8s.af, read one by one, including the conference talks through their captions, and 24 write-ups from 2023 onward. Companies cited with a year in `why` but without a link now have one or were removed, and two webhook checks no longer cite a Skyscanner incident that was about Argo CD pruning. `docs/CHECKS.md` lists every case and says how many checks have one. Rubric 0.1.53. ([#28](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/28))

### Fixed

- ConfigMap values survived in two annotations after `data` was scrubbed: `kubectl.kubernetes.io/last-applied-configuration`, which `kubectl apply` fills with the whole object, and the k3s helm-controller's `objectset.rio.cattle.io/applied`, the same copy gzip-compressed and base64-encoded. On a k3s snapshot 29 of 89 ConfigMaps carried 287 KB of raw values that way, 27 through the first annotation and 2 through the second. Snapshots written before this fix still hold them. Both annotation values are now replaced by their length and shape; the keys stay, `kubegap_scrub.annotations_dropped` lists them, and secret-like keys in the copy are counted. The same two annotations are dropped from a Secret in the defensive path, although Secrets are never fetched. ([#50](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/50))
- Image sizes are reported with the right unit. `crictl images` sizes are decimal (`1.23GB` is 10⁹ bytes), so `lib/nodeos.py` no longer converts them with 1024 steps, and lowercase `kB` sizes are no longer dropped. The ECR summary's unlabeled tuples, once misread by a report as a "96 GB image", are labeled rows with the unit in the key: `largest` became `largest_repos[{name, gib}]` and `repos_stale_pull` became `[{name, gib, days_since_pull}]`. The `ecr.stale-storage` and `node.*` image evidence now writes GiB/MiB, and `references/report-writing.md` says to never write a number without its unit. Fixed by [@MinwooPark93](https://github.com/MinwooPark93) in [#48](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/pull/48). Rubric 0.1.55. ([#10](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/10))

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-02

The first milestone, permissions and the read-only claim, is complete: issues #1 to #5. Contributors to this release: [@junhoeKu](https://github.com/junhoeKu) (#31) and [@Rapil3090](https://github.com/Rapil3090) (#34).

### Removed

- The `helm-values` collection step and the check `hygiene.helm-release-pure-defaults`. `helm get values` reads Helm's release Secrets and the values often hold credentials; they were stored raw in the snapshot. `helm list` stays, and the READMEs now say that it reads Helm's release records, which Helm keeps in Secrets, and that only their metadata is stored. When `helm list` fails for lack of secrets permission, the summary says so instead of "helm not installed". Rubric 0.1.52, 402 checks. ([#3](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/3))

### Changed

- Node SSH connections check host keys strictly (`StrictHostKeyChecking=yes`): only nodes already in `~/.ssh/known_hosts` are reached, and a refused key is reported with a hint. `ssh.accept_new_hosts: true` in config.yaml restores the earlier `accept-new` behaviour. `deploy/node-access/README.md` describes the preparation. ([#4](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/4))
- EKS node commands run through a custom SSM document, `kubegap-node-read` (`deploy/permissions/ssm-document.yaml`), instead of `AWS-RunShellScript`. The document embeds every read command and takes no free command text; kubegap compares its sha256 with the account copy before sending and `kubegap setup` uploads it with consent (`--install-ssm-document`, config `ssm_document`). The IAM policy allows `ssm:SendCommand` only on that document and only on instances tagged `kubernetes.io/cluster/<CLUSTER_NAME>=owned`. ([#1](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/1))
- `kubegap nodes --cmd` is no longer available on the SSM backend; `--step <name>` runs one documented step instead.

- ConfigMap values are no longer written to the snapshot. `objects/configmaps.json` keeps each key's length and shape, the real value only for the configuration keys kubegap parses (`KEEP_VALUES` in `lib/scrub.py`), and a `kubegap_scrub` record with the secret-like key names that `security.plaintext-configmap-secret` now reads. ([#2](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/2))
- Component pod logs are deleted from the snapshot after `summarize` has aggregated them; `logs/pods/REMOVED.txt` marks it and `logs/pods/AGGREGATED.json` keeps the log-derived results so a rerun of `summarize` gives the same summary. Sample lines kept in `summary.logs` are masked. `keep_component_logs: true` in config.yaml keeps the raw logs. ([#2](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/2))

### Fixed

- The DaemonSet node backend (`deploy/node-access/daemonset.yaml`) was never used by collection: the node step gate and the pod-metrics node-curl fallback accepted only `ssh` and `ssm`, so every node step was skipped as "no node command backend". Both now accept `daemonset`. `node_access: daemonset` in config.yaml is applied like the other values and says so when no reader pod is Running. The `get` and `create` permissions on `pods/exec` that `kubectl exec` needs are in a new, namespaced `deploy/permissions/role-daemonset-exec.yaml`. ([#5](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/5))
- The embedded test cases of `platform.k8s-version-eol` compared against the real date and began failing once Kubernetes 1.34 passed its end-of-support date, which turned CI red for every pull request. Test cases can now pin the date with `today` in their inventory; `days_until` reads it.
- The adjustment table in `llm/adjustments.md` now also records every aggregate the agent recounted against the first pass, in its own `Recounted aggregates` table, so a number in the report that differs from `findings-draft.json` always has a reason next to it. Reported and fixed by [@junhoeKu](https://github.com/junhoeKu) in [#31](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/pull/31). ([#13](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/13))
- The password-line pattern of `security.plaintext-configmap-secret` missed lines whose key is the bare word, such as `password = ...`; it required one leading character.

### Added

- `scripts/kubegap prune --keep-daily N --keep-full M` removes old snapshot folders of a context, with defaults from `retention` in config.yaml and `--dry-run` to list first. The latest complete daily and full snapshots always stay because they are the diff baselines; interrupted collections are removed unless one is still running. ([#20](https://github.com/KyungHwanKim-devs/kubegap/issues/20))
- `deploy/node-access/COMMANDS.md`, the complete list of commands kubegap runs on nodes per backend, generated by `scripts/kubegap node-commands --markdown` and kept current by a test.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- First release.
- Read-only audit of a Kubernetes cluster against 403 checks in 12 areas, defined as data in `collectors/*/checks.yaml` with embedded test cases.
- Two modes: `daily` with a reduced collection, compared with the previous daily run, and `full` with everything, compared with the previous full run.
- Two report files: `report.md` written for a person, with the top problems and why each happens, and `report-details.md` assembled from the snapshot.
- EKS as the baseline platform and k3s as a variant, with node access through SSM, SSH or a DaemonSet.
- Expectation profiles `prod`, `lab`, `small-prod`, `multi-az-prod` and `dev`.
- English and Korean README and sample reports.
